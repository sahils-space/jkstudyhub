1. Legal Compliance Framework

Under India's Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020, blanket "No Return / No Refund" disclaimers are legally void for goods delivered in a defective, damaged, or incorrect state. JK Study Hub maintains a transparent, category-wise fulfillment standard that balances customer rights with operational viability.

3. Detailed Category Guidelines

Category 1: NCERT & JKBOSE Textbooks / Academic Books

  • Pre-Dispatch: Orders can be cancelled at zero cost before physical dispatch occurs.
  • Post-Delivery: Returns or physical exchanges are accepted strictly within 48 hours of delivery for manufacturing flaws (missing or unprinted pages) or incorrect fulfillment. Books must remain unread, unmarked, and structurally untampered with.

Category 2: Backpacks, Fine Stationery & Desk Gear

  • Pre-Dispatch: Full cancellation allowed prior to shipment packaging.
  • Post-Delivery: Defective items (faulty zippers, torn fabric, missing hardware) can be submitted for return or replacement within 7 days of delivery. All tags, brand cards, and original wrapping must remain intact.

Category 3: Customised & Personalised Merchandise

  • Production Gate: Production begins upon written customer approval of the design preview.
  • Non-Refundable Status: Once production commences, cancellations, returns, or refunds for change of mind are strictly barred under standard statutory exceptions for custom goods.
  • Remedial Claims: If an item arrives structurally damaged or displays an engraving error attributable to JK Study Hub (differing from the approved preview), a replacement will be dispatched at no additional cost.

4. Mandatory 360° Unboxing Video Rule

Mandatory Unboxing Video Protocol for Transit Claims

To prevent fraudulent claims and establish clear accountability for transit damage or short-shipments, the following protocol is mandatory:

  • Requirement: The customer must record a single, continuous, unedited 360-degree unboxing video when opening the parcel.
  • Verification Steps: The video must clearly show the unopened outer box/bag, the shipping label with tracking details, the unbroken packaging seal, and the continuous opening of the parcel revealing the contents.
  • Submission Timeline: The unedited video must be transmitted to WhatsApp (+91 96226 05714) within 24 to 48 hours of carrier delivery confirmation. Claims submitted without this video proof cannot be processed for transit damage or missing items.

5. Refund Processing Timelines & Modes

  • Verification: Returned items undergo physical inspection at the Pattan Hub within 48 hours of arrival.
  • COD Refunds: Approved refunds for Cash on Delivery orders are issued directly via UPI transfer or direct NEFT bank deposit to the customer's verified bank account.
  • Turnaround Time: Upon approval, refunds are processed within 5 to 7 working days.
