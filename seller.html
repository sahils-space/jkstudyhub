Secret Wholesale Sourcing Directory & Sourcing Playbook

Private guide on where to buy copies & books at factory prices to guarantee high profit margins.

1. Delhi – Nai Sarak & Chawri Bazar Top Factory Hub

India's largest paper & notebook manufacturing cluster. Exercise copies cost ₹18–₹26 wholesale (which shops sell for ₹50–₹60). Novels cost ₹70–₹110 (sell for ₹200–₹290).

Cargo: Daily transport trucks directly to Parimpora Srinagar (~₹2/copy freight).
2. Srinagar – Maisuma & Red Cross Road Local Kashmir Hub

Central wholesale stationery market in Srinagar. Ideal for fast restocks in lots of 50–100 pieces without travelling to Delhi.

Margin: 25% to 35% discount on MRP for carton purchases.
3. Parimpora Cargo Depots Logistics

Major cargo carriers receiving parcels from Delhi/Jalandhar: TCI Express, Kashmir Golden Transport, V-Trans & ARC.

Delivery Time: 3 to 5 days transit from Delhi to Srinagar.
