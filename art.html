Slow Handcraft

The 4-Step Resin Alchemy

Why hand-poured epoxy resin requires patience, chemistry, and artistic precision.

01

Botanical Curation

Real Kashmiri Chinar leaves and garden roses are harvested, pressed in wooden presses, and moisture-extracted for 3 weeks to prevent discoloration.

02

Vacuum Casting

Optical-grade, non-toxic epoxy resin is hand-mixed with metallic micas and degassed to eliminate micro-bubbles before pouring into molds.

03

48-Hour Slow Cure

Unlike commercial plastic, true artisan resin cures slowly in a dust-free chamber, creating a glass-like weight and superior heat tolerance.

04

Diamond Polish

Every piece is sanded up to 3000-grit, hand-beveled with gold leaf, and packed in eco-luxe gift wrapping with a custom wax seal.
