Part 2: Privacy Policy

Comprehensive Privacy Governance • Updated Academic Session 2026–2027

1. Information Collected

JK Study Hub collects only the minimum personal data necessary to facilitate commercial transactions, fulfill logistics, and deliver customized products.

  • Customer Contact Details: Full legal name, primary telephone number, WhatsApp contact number, email address, billing address, and physical shipping address.
  • Transaction Records: Historical purchase logs, payment methods used (COD / UPI / Card), invoice copies, and communication logs regarding support queries.
  • Personalized Customisation Data: Digital images, photographic artwork, custom phrases, personal names, dates, or messages transmitted to us for rendering onto personalized items.

2. Purpose and Use of Collected Information

Collected data is deployed strictly to execute essential operational tasks:

  • Processing orders, issuing tax invoices, and verifying delivery addresses.
  • Coordinating localized dispatch via in-house delivery staff or courier partners.
  • Rendering physical customizations (engraving, printing, resin casting) per customer specifications.
  • Providing real-time WhatsApp updates for dispatch, tracking, and customer support.

3. Handling of Custom Photos & Personalised Data

We maintain strict data privacy controls regarding personal media provided for customized items:

Strict Protection of Customer Media

  • Restricted Access: Customer photos, personal images, and custom inscriptions are accessible exclusively by authorized production personnel for printing or laser engraving.
  • No Unsolicited Usage: Personal photos are never published on marketing channels, social media pages, portfolio galleries, or third-party platforms without explicit, documented consent from the customer.
  • Data Lifespan: Source media files uploaded for custom items are permanently scrubbed from our active production drives within thirty (30) days of order delivery.

4. Data Sharing & Security Safeguards

  • Logistics Partners: Address and phone data are shared solely with assigned courier services or local dispatch riders to ensure accurate product delivery.
  • Zero Commercial Exploitation: Customer contact lists, shopping habits, and personal details are never sold, rented, monetized, or traded to third-party marketing organizations.
  • Data Security: Personal data stored digitally is protected through encrypted communication channels and access-controlled databases to prevent unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure.

5. Customer Privacy Rights & Contact

Customers maintain full authority over their stored personal data.

  • Access & Erasure Rights: Customers may request a copy of their stored personal data or request the immediate deletion of their contact details and custom photos from our servers upon completion of order delivery.
  • Submission Channel: Direct all data rights requests to info.jkstudyhub@gmail.com or via WhatsApp at +91 96226 05714.
